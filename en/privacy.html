Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026

What this covers

This policy describes the La Sainte-Flanelle iPhone scoreboard and this static information website. The community at lasainteflanelle.ca has separate accounts and policies. The app now focuses on scores, schedules, statistics, celebrations and widgets; social screens have been removed.

Browsing and game data

You can view scores, the schedule and statistics without signing in. The app retrieves game information through its Appwrite service and public NHL data endpoints. Requests to these services can include normal network information, such as your IP address. Game data may be delayed, corrected or unavailable.

Your account and notifications

If you sign in with Apple, the service uses your Apple account identifier and an app session. The app does not request your Apple name or email. When supported background alerts are enabled, the service stores a device notification token, installation identifier, game subscriptions and relevant language, sound and delay choices. Live Activities may also use Apple-issued update tokens. Background goal and final-score alerts are supported by the deployed service after Sign in with Apple, notification permission and successful registration. Delivery depends on the game-data source, network and Apple. Notification permission is controlled in iOS.

On your iPhone

The app stores language, time-zone, sound, accessibility and reminder preferences locally. Its revocable session is stored in Keychain. The app and widget extension share a read-only game-data cache and relevant display preferences through an App Group; account sessions and private profile data are not shared with widgets. iOS controls widget refresh timing. No advertising or cross-app tracking SDK is included.

Service providers

Appwrite Cloud hosts authentication, account and service data in the New York region, United States. Apple handles Sign in with Apple, notifications, widgets and Live Activities. Cloudflare runs the shared game-ingestion scheduler. GitHub Pages hosts this static website and receives normal web requests. Support email is handled through iCloud Mail. These providers process information under their own policies. This website has no analytics, contact form or advertising cookies.

Earlier community data

Removing social screens does not automatically delete data from previous app versions. Existing account records may include a display name, avatar, optional city and biography, contributions, uploaded photos, RSVPs, check-ins, reactions, blocks and reports. The account-deletion process continues to handle these records. The separate lasainteflanelle.ca community is unaffected.

Retention and deletion

Account records remain until deletion or applicable maintenance. Automated maintenance targets push-event and delivery-attempt records after 24 hours, push baselines and subscriptions after seven days without an update, and expired sign-in nonces after one hour. Legacy cleanup targets include check-ins after 48 hours, reports and moderation audit records after 90 days, unattached uploads after 24 hours and removed content after 30 days. Bounded cleanup, backlogs or outages can delay these targets. Account deletion revokes Apple authorization and removes account data, legacy contributions and notification subscriptions in resumable batches. Minimal retry records can remain until cleanup succeeds. Provider logs and backups follow provider retention practices; support emails are handled separately.

Your choices and contact

Use Settings to change preferences or sign out. If signed in, choose Settings → Delete account to request deletion. Email ssmk.consulting@icloud.com for access, correction, deletion help or privacy concerns. We may need to verify account control. Never send passwords, session tokens or identity documents. This policy will be updated when collection practices change.
