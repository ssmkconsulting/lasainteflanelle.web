Privacy policy

What this covers

This policy describes the La Sainte-Flanelle iPhone app and this static information website. The separate community at lasainteflanelle.ca has its own accounts and policies. Updated November 5, 2026.

Your information

The app stores your Apple account identifier, display name, chosen avatar, optional city and biography; contributions and photos; event RSVPs, self-reported game check-ins, reactions, blocks and reports. It does not request your Apple name or email, GPS location, contacts or precise seat. Do not put private details in public contributions. Support emails contain whatever you choose to send.

What is visible

Approved profiles, contributions, photos and public event details can be viewed without signing in. Pending contributions are visible to their author and authorized moderators. Blocking hides content between the two accounts, but cannot prevent someone from browsing public content while signed out. The app displays aggregate participation counts, not private attendee contact details.

Where information goes

Appwrite Cloud hosts authentication sessions, the community database and private photo storage in the New York region, United States. The server verifies Sign in with Apple tokens with Apple. Files have no public storage permission; the app API checks access before returning a photo. Cloudflare runs a minute-by-minute scheduler that sends a service request to Appwrite; it does not receive community records or device tokens. Apple provides device notification and photo-picker services. When game alerts are enabled, Appwrite stores your device notification token, installation identifier, per-game choices, language, sound and delay, and sends the token and a short game alert to Apple’s push notification service. GitHub Pages hosts this static site and receives normal web requests. Support email goes to our iCloud inbox. These providers process network information such as IP addresses under their own policies.

On your iPhone

Your revocable app session is stored in Keychain and is not synchronized to other devices. Celebration preferences, reminder choices and game-event deduplication state are stored locally. Selecting a photo shares only the selected image; the app and server resize or re-encode it to strip image metadata. Local reminders require notification permission. No advertising or tracking SDK is included.

Retention and deletion

Active profiles and contributions remain while the account exists. Automated maintenance targets expired sign-in nonces after one hour, check-ins after 48 hours, reports and moderator audit records after 90 days, unattached uploads after 24 hours, removed content after 30 days, push events and delivery-attempt records after 24 hours, and push baselines/subscriptions after seven days without an update. Cleanup is bounded and runs hourly, so backlogs or outages can delay these targets. Account deletion revokes Apple authorization and removes the profile, contributions, media, RSVPs, check-ins, reactions, blocks, reports and notification subscriptions in resumable batches. Replies attached to deleted threads are removed. Minimal deletion retry markers remain only until cleanup succeeds. Infrastructure logs and backups follow the hosting providers’ retention practices; support emails are handled separately.

Your choices

Edit your profile, remove contributions and check-ins, cancel an RSVP, block users, or request account deletion in the app. Contact ssmk.consulting@icloud.com for access, correction, deletion help or privacy concerns. Do not send passwords or identity documents. We may need to verify that you control an account before acting on a request.

Game data and changes

A permitted game-data provider has not yet been connected. Background goal notifications are disabled in this build. This policy will be updated before adding a new provider or changing collection practices. No sale of personal data or cross-app advertising tracking is implemented.
