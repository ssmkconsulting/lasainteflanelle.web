Terms of use

Updated November 7, 2026

An independent fan scoreboard

La Sainte-Flanelle is an independent iPhone app for Montréal hockey fans. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the NHL or the Montréal Canadiens. Team marks belong to their respective owners. These terms cover the app; the existing community at lasainteflanelle.ca remains separate.

Scores and availability

Scores, statistics, clocks, widgets and alerts depend on external services and iOS delivery. They may be delayed, stale, corrected or unavailable. A widget is a periodically refreshed view, not a continuous live feed. Celebration timing may differ from a television broadcast. The service may change or be interrupted, including when hosting limits are reached.

Your preferences and account

You can use the scoreboard without an account. Keep any account you use secure. Reminders, notifications, sounds, lighting and haptics depend on your settings and device permissions. Full celebration songs play while the app is in the foreground. Game Night screen-awake behavior applies while the app is open.

Current release status

The app is in TestFlight testing and has no public App Store download yet. Background goal and final-score alerts still require a server update. No purchases, ticket transactions or betting features are offered.

Previous versions

Social posting, meetups and profile editing are no longer part of the app interface. Previously stored account data can still be removed through account deletion. Removing these screens does not delete your separate lasainteflanelle.ca account.
