Delete your app account

Updated November 9, 2026

In the app

Open Settings → Delete account while signed in. Confirm that you want permanent deletion, then reauthenticate with Sign in with Apple. Keep the app open while the request is processed. If the connection fails, return to Delete account to continue.

What is removed

The deletion process removes your app account data and notification subscriptions. It also handles records from earlier app versions: profile, contributions, uploaded photos, RSVPs, check-ins, reactions, blocks and reports. Replies attached to threads you created are removed. Apple authorization for this app is revoked. Minimal retry records can remain until cleanup completes.

Separate accounts and local settings

This does not delete your Apple account, your account or contributions on lasainteflanelle.ca, or emails sent to support. Local sound, language and other preferences may remain on your phone until you remove the app. Logs and backups follow hosting-provider retention practices.

Using the app as a guest?

No app account is created just by browsing scores, schedules and statistics. Manage notification permission in iOS Settings and local preferences in the app.

Need help?

Email ssmk.consulting@icloud.com with “App account deletion” in the subject. We may ask you to verify account control. Never send your Apple password, a session token or an identity document.
