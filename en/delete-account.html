Delete your app account

In the app

Open Profile → Delete account. Read the explanation, confirm that you want permanent deletion, and reauthenticate with Sign in with Apple. Keep the app open while it processes the request. If the connection fails, return to Delete account to continue.

What is removed

Your app profile, contributions, uploaded photos, RSVPs, game check-ins, reactions, blocks and reports are removed. Replies in threads you created are also removed. Your Apple authorization for this app is revoked. A minimal retry record can remain until authentication cleanup completes.

Separate accounts and local settings

This does not delete your account or contributions on lasainteflanelle.ca, your Apple account, or messages you sent to support. Local celebration settings can remain on your phone until you remove the app. Hosting logs and backups follow provider retention practices.

Need help?

Email ssmk.consulting@icloud.com with “App account deletion” in the subject. We may ask you to verify account control. Never send your Apple password, a session token or an identity document. The app is in preparation; real Apple sign-in and deletion remain gated until Apple configuration is complete.
