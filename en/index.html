03BEFORE PUCK DROP

Good to know.

A fan-built app with its heart on the ice. Here’s where things stand.

Get in touch ↗
Can I download the app?

The app is being tested through TestFlight. It is not yet publicly available on the App Store. Public availability will be announced here.

Do I need an account?

You can browse the schedule, scores and statistics, and preview celebrations without an account. Some background services need Sign in with Apple and the relevant iOS permissions.

Are goal alerts available?

Yes. Goal and final-score alerts are supported by the deployed service after Sign in with Apple, iOS notification permission and successful registration. Enable each alert type in Settings. Choose one pregame reminder: 15, 10 or 5 minutes. Apple and the game-data source affect delivery; alert timing is not guaranteed.

What about Live Activities?

Start a Live Activity from Settings to follow the matchup on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island on supported iPhones. Its team regions share the opponent’s palette, stripes and chrome finish, with away-first ordering and upcoming game time. Stale live data asks you to open the app to refresh; background updates depend on the service, connection and iOS.

Can I match the celebration to my broadcast?

Set a foreground celebration delay from 0 to 120 seconds. It affects songs, lighting and haptics, without delaying the scoreboard or background alerts. The feed and your broadcast can have different delays, so exact TV synchronization is not guaranteed. Leaving the app or a genuine audio interruption stops full-song playback.

What do LIVE FEED ON and OFF mean?

The app shows a green symbol with LIVE FEED ON when the game feed is fresh, and a red symbol with LIVE FEED OFF for stale or unavailable data and final or postponed games. Text makes the state clear without relying on color. It is a feed-status display; Game Night controls sounds and the display separately.

Where can I find the season schedule?

Home keeps the focus on the game. Open Schedule from the SF shield menu for the full season, home/away filters and your preferred time zone.

What happened to the community features?

The app now focuses on the Canadiens scoreboard. Discussions, photos, social profiles and meetups are no longer part of its interface. The existing community at lasainteflanelle.ca remains separate.
