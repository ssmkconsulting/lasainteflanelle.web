03BEFORE PUCK DROP

Good to know.

A fan-built app with its heart on the ice. Here’s where things stand.

Get in touch ↗
Can I download the app?

The app is being tested through TestFlight. It is not yet publicly available on the App Store. Public availability will be announced here.

Do I need an account?

You can browse the schedule, scores and statistics, and preview celebrations without an account. Some background services need Sign in with Apple and the relevant iOS permissions.

Are goal alerts available?

Pregame reminders are available, with 15, 10 or 5 minute options. Background goal and final-score alerts still need a server update. Full celebration songs play while the app is in the foreground.

What about Live Activities?

The app integrates scores on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones. Background updates depend on service availability and are not guaranteed to run continuously.

What happened to the community features?

The app now focuses on the Canadiens scoreboard. Discussions, photos, social profiles and meetups are no longer part of its interface. The existing community at lasainteflanelle.ca remains separate.
