About the community

Updated November 9, 2026

The app has a new focus

La Sainte-Flanelle now focuses on the Canadiens scoreboard: scores, the season schedule, statistics, celebrations and widgets. Social discussions, photos, profiles, meetups and moderation screens are no longer part of the iPhone app.

The existing website remains separate

The community at lasainteflanelle.ca remains a distinct service, with its own accounts and policies. This change to the iPhone app does not change that community.

Data from earlier app versions

Removing social screens does not automatically delete earlier account data. See Privacy or Delete your app account for information and help.
