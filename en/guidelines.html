Community guidelines

Cheer loudly. Treat people kindly.

No harassment, hate, threats, explicit sexual content, spam, ticket sales, gambling promotion or impersonation. Critique the play without attacking the person.

Share responsibly

Post photos only if you have the right to share them and permission from the people pictured. Never share private contact information, home addresses, precise seats or another person’s personal details. Do not post illegal material.

Meet in public

Use public venues or a city when organizing a meetup. Attendance and check-ins are self-reported. The app does not verify participants or guarantee the safety of an event. Make your own arrangements and do not request private attendee contact details.

Moderation and reporting

All contributions, profile edits, photos and messages await moderator approval. Basic automatic filters reject certain unsafe text; moderators make publication decisions. Use the report menu on content or a profile and block users when needed. Moderators may remove content or suspend an account. Contact support to raise a concern or ask about a moderation decision.

A shared space

Accept these rules before contributing. Respect moderators and other fans. There are no anonymous random chats, direct messages or follower systems in this release. Contact ssmk.consulting@icloud.com for help.
